00:00 — About

Every channel has a voice. My job is finding it before the first cut.

I've spent the last 4+ years editing for digital, television, OTT and YouTube — and freelancing since 2021 across 1,000+ projects. I work across Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro and After Effects, but the tool matters less than the read: pacing, audio, colour and story, tuned to how your specific audience watches.

I also shoot — so edits are informed by lighting, framing and camera angles from the very first setup, not bolted on afterward. The goal isn't just a polished video. It's a channel identity that holds together, edit after edit.
